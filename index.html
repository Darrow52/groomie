Still Have Questions?

Will this irritate my skin?

EverBlume™ is a capsule you take with water, so it never touches your skin. Our skincare and LED devices are dermatologist-tested and made without parabens, sulfates or added fragrance. If your skin is very sensitive, try a new product on a small area first and wait 24 hours.

How soon will I see results?

Hair grows about 1 cm a month, so results build gradually. Most women notice less shedding after 60–90 days and visibly fuller hair by month three. For the best, lasting results, take EverBlume™ every day for at least six months.

Is it safe to use every day?

Yes. EverBlume™ is designed for daily use: two capsules a day with a meal. It's vegan, gluten-free and non-GMO, and made in a cGMP-certified facility. If you're pregnant, nursing or taking medication, check with your doctor before starting any supplement.

What if it's not right for me?

We want you to love your routine. If you're not happy with your first order, contact our team within 30 days and we'll help you find a better fit or arrange a refund. See our Return Policy for details.

How do I clean my device?

After each use, wipe the device with a soft, lint-free cloth lightly dampened with water or an alcohol-free cleanser. Never rinse it under the tap or submerge it, and let it dry completely before storing it in its pouch.
